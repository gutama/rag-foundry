# Product Guidelines: RAG Foundry

## Brand Identity & Tone
- **Tone:** Authoritative, precise, objective, and security-conscious.
- **Voice:** Professional, institutional, transparent, and evidence-based. Responses must always cite exact source evidence and clearly state limitations or abstain when confidence is low.

## User Experience (UX) Principles
- **Document-Centric UI:** Provide seamless navigation between chat answers, document collections, metadata schemas, and native document viewers with visual bounding boxes / citations.
- **Explicit Provenance & Citations:** Every claim or generated answer must display direct links to source documents, page numbers, and exact text excerpts.
- **Governed & Audited Operations:** Show governance state (e.g., assertion candidate vs. verified concept, metadata validation errors) clearly to curators and administrators.
- **Feedback & Curation:** Enable inline feedback, entity resolution approval, and OKF export workflows for domain experts.

## Visual & Technical Standards
- **Interface Aesthetics:** Clean, modern, high-contrast dark/light themes designed for information-dense intelligence displays (graphs, tables, facets).
- **Performance & Responsiveness:** Streaming API responses for LLM answers, fast facet filtering, and non-blocking background job updates.
